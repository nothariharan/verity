## 1. Apple HIG Design Language & Presentation Aesthetics

Verity’s visual identity rejects chaotic hackathon slide templates crammed with tiny bullet points. Instead, it follows Apple’s signature Keynote aesthetic:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                       APPLE HIG DESIGN SPECIFICATIONS                       │
├─────────────────────┬───────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Background Canvas   │ Deep OLED Space `#05070B` with subtle `#0D1117` cards  │
│ Primary Typography  │ SF Pro Display / Apple San Francisco (Bold Headlines) │
│ Secondary Body      │ SF Pro Text / Regular, 1.4 Line Height, Clean White   │
│ Primary Accent      │ Emerald Glow `#10B981` (The "Owned" Hypothesis)       │
│ Secondary Accent    │ Electric Blue `#3B82F6` (The "Contributed" Hypothesis)│
│ State / Entropy     │ Warm Amber `#F59E0B` (Under Investigation)            │
│ Border & Materials  │ 1px Subsurface Glass Border (`rgba(255,255,255,0.08)`│
│ Spatial Rhythm      │ 8pt Spatial Grid, Generous Negative Space (≥ 40%)     │
└─────────────────────┴───────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Slide 2: Problem Statement and Approach
* **Official Template Header:** `PROBLEM STATEMENT AND APPROACH`
* **Visual Layout:** Two high-contrast side-by-side cards with glassmorphic borders.
  * **Card Left (The Broken Status Quo):**  
    * Headline: *"The 2.5-Second Monologue Trap"*
    * Bullets: Cold 1-way recorded videos (54% drop-off) · Scripted questions easily gamed by off-screen AI copilots · Unjust, unauditable black-box percentages.
  * **Card Right (Verity's Approach):**  
    * Headline: *"The Socratic Investigation"*
    * Bullets: Every claim becomes an active case · 3 competing hypotheses: Owned, Contributed, Surface · Verity asks the one question that separates the tied pair · Every judgment leaves an immutable receipt.
* **Speaker Script:**
  > *"Hiring today is broken. Asynchronous video tools force candidates to talk to a countdown timer, driving over 54% of top engineers to quit the application. Meanwhile, candidates use off-screen AI copilots to read answers to standard behavioral trivia.*  
  > *Verity replaces quizzes with an investigation. Every project on a resume becomes a case. We don't ask textbook trivia; we probe the boundary between what the candidate personally designed and what they merely used. And every belief shift is anchored to verifiable evidence."*

### Slide 3: Feasibility (Technical, Financial & Operational)
* **Official Template Header:** `FEASIBILITY`
* **Visual Layout:** Three distinct vertical columns with bold hero metrics.
  * **Column 1: Technical (The Duplex Voice Floor):**  
    * Metric: `< 600ms Latency`  
    * Subtext: Decoupled Floor vs. Mind · Speculative pre-drafting eliminates turn delay · Sub-120ms smart barge-in.
  * **Column 2: Financial (Unit Economics):**  
    * Metric: `$0.10 / Interview`  
    * Subtext: Pure API architecture (Deepgram + ElevenLabs + Groq) · 75x cheaper than 3D avatar bots ($7.50/call) requiring server GPUs.
  * **Column 3: Operational (Zero Friction):**  
    * Metric: `100% Browser Native`  
    * Subtext: Zero installs, zero kernel drivers · Complete offline testing mode with deterministic fakes (`PROVIDERS=fake`).
* **Speaker Script:**
  > *"Is this feasible today? Absolutely. We don't rely on unreleased research models or expensive server GPUs. By decoupling the Voice Floor from the Intelligence Mind, we pre-draft the next question while the candidate is still speaking, driving latency down under 600 milliseconds.*  
  > *Because our computer vision runs locally in the browser via WebAssembly, our total infrastructure cost is just 10 cents for a full 15-minute technical interview—75 times cheaper than avatar bots."*

## 3. Anticipated Judges' Questions & Bulletproof Q&A Defenses

| Question | The Attack Angle | The Bulletproof Defense |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **"Why not just use OpenAI's Realtime Speech-to-Speech API?"** | Testing architectural independence. | *"Speech-to-speech models decide their own words, hallucinate factual claims, and cannot be bound to an immutable audit log. Verity's planner commits the exact question as text before audio is synthesized, ensuring 100% deterministic evaluation."* |
| **"What if the candidate has an accent or speaks slowly?"** | Testing algorithmic bias. | *"Verity's belief updates depend strictly on text transcripts—never audio pitch, inflection, or cadence. Forgetting is not penalized, and our VAD features dynamic energy hysteresis."* |
| **"Can a candidate cheat by using ChatGPT on another screen?"** | Testing integrity robustness. | *"Verity's Socratic questions probe private architectural trade-offs that generic copilots cannot answer without multi-second delays. Furthermore, our edge saccade FFT detects the tell-tale periodic sawtooth wave of reading off auxiliary displays."* |
