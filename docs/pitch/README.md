## ⚡ The Presenter's 30-Second Quick Cheat Sheet

* **The 1-Line North Star:**  
  > *"Resumes make claims. Verity checks them, out loud."*
* **The Core Metaphor:**  
  Verity is an **investigation of claims by an elite detective**, not an automated quiz.
* **The Three Hypotheses:**  
  **Owned** (they built it and know the trade-offs) vs. **Contributed** (they used it under someone else's architecture) vs. **Surface** (they know the vocabulary, not the work).
* **The Demo Climax:**  
  The case ring swings mid-sentence $\to$ locks solid green when they finish $\to$ clicking the receipt plays the exact 10-second audio snippet.
* **The Cost Metric:**  
  **$0.10 per interview** (75x cheaper than $7.50 3D avatar cloud GPUs).
* **The Legal Defense:**  
  **Receipts, not scores.** SHA-256 tamper-evident hash chain compliant with NYC Local Law 144 and EEOC regulations.
