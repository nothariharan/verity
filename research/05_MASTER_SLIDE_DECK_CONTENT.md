## Slide 2: Problem Statement and Approach

* **Slide Heading:** **PROBLEM STATEMENT AND APPROACH**
* **The Tri-Fold Market Crisis:**
  1. **Recruiter Fatigue & Inconsistency:** 70% of recruiter time is wasted on repetitive initial screens; identical candidate responses are graded arbitrarily depending on human interviewer mood and unconscious bias.
  2. **Candidate Dehumanization & Drop-off:** Over 54% of candidates abandon legacy one-way video interviews (e.g. HireVue); candidates receive zero constructive feedback.
  3. **The Remote Cheating Epidemic:** 30%+ of remote interviews now suffer from candidates reading live LLM answers from off-screen copilots (Final Round AI, ChatGPT overlays), while legacy proctoring tools act like invasive spyware.
* **Our Unified Socratic Approach:**
  * **Pillar 1: Profile-Aware Contextual Engine:** Ingests candidate resume PDF and Target Job Description to synthesize a personalized, deep-probing interview trajectory.
  * **Pillar 2: Conversational Socratic Dialogue:** Ultra-low latency voice agent (<600ms) that actively listens, probes deeper when a candidate claims expertise, and offers scaffolding when they struggle.
  * **Pillar 3: On-Device Integrity Guardian:** Google MediaPipe FaceLandmarker running via WebAssembly directly in the candidate's browser to detect multi-screen cheating with **zero cloud video storage**.
